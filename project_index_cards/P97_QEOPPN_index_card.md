## B. Inward-facing side

### B1. Methodological significance

| Field | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `headline_finding` | The partners kept several location descriptors (Stratford, E20, East Village and others) rather than a single identity; interviews and the evidence review agreed on the main differences: use of the Olympic and Paralympic legacy, one destination or several, and proximity and connectivity. The evidence review adds that over a dozen names were in use for the Park and its districts, that interviewees valued the university and V&A research presence more than the consumer offer, and that cost pressure on Hackney Wick creative businesses (costs up tenfold in ten years, one interviewee) was a named risk.
