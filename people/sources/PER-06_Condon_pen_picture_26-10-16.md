Brian Condon, PhD – Economic impact and methodological review

Brian Condon is a technology strategist and researcher with over 35 years' experience in innovation, public policy and economic impact assessment. He combines quantitative analysis with a practical understanding of how technologies, organisations and places interact, and has extensive experience reviewing complex evidence and methodologies.

Brian founded and directed the University of London's Centre for Creative Collaboration (C4CC), a partnership of four universities supporting innovation among creative practitioners, researchers, freelancers and businesses. His work included the London Creative and Digital Fusion programme and economic impact studies of creative and digital infrastructure in several UK cities and regions.

Through Networks.coop, Brian works on the economic and social impacts of place-based digital infrastructure. Recent projects include digital inclusion in Preston, covering programme design and monitoring arrangements, and co-operative digital infrastructure in Greater Manchester, examining governance, investment, business models and community benefit.

This complements his wider experience developing technical evidence, modelling assumptions and methods for government economic impact assessments. His particular strength is testing how evidence and modelling choices affect reported impacts and the confidence placed in them.

Brian serves on the advisory board of [PlaceMUS XR](https://placemus-xr-eccch.eu/), an EU-funded research programme exploring digital technologies for the interpretation and experience of musical heritage. His contribution includes independent scrutiny of primary research and assessment of the financial viability, funding and implementation of heritage technologies.

For the V&A study, he brings experience of creative-sector innovation, economic analysis, evaluation design and independent methodological review, particularly around additionality, sampling, cost attribution and the reliability of impact estimates.
