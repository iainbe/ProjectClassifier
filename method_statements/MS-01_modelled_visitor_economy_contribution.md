## Bid-ready paragraph (generic; fill the [slots])

For [project and client] we estimated the annual economic contribution of [the offer] to [the place] using a transparent attribution model. We divided visitors into groups using official tourism estimates, applied stated judgements on how many engage with the offer and how much of their spending depends on it, and deducted a reserve for spending not received locally and an allowance for displacement. We converted the remainder to gross value added using a local conversion and added supplier and household effects with a stated multiplier. Every assumption is visible and tested one at a time. The result is a modelled estimate, not a measured impact.

## Steps
1. Split visitors into groups using official volume and spend estimates.
2. Apply two stated judgements per group: share who engage, and share of spending that depends on the offer.
3. Deduct a stated reserve for spending not securely received inside the boundary.
4. Deduct a separate displacement allowance.
5. Convert to GVA with a local visitor-economy conversion.
6. Add supplier and household effects with a stated multiplier.
7. Test one assumption at a time; present scenarios, not confidence intervals.

## Data needed
Official visit and spend estimates for the boundary geography, operator and trade returns, stakeholder interviews, and a GVA conversion and multiplier with a stated source.

## What we cannot claim
- A measured or causal impact, or a confidence range.
- An independent split between indirect and induced effects.
- Growth or decline against an earlier study that used a different measure.
- Any uplift from future events that has not been evidenced.
