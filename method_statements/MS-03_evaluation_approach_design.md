## When to use
When a buyer asks how we would design a mixed-methods impact evaluation that distinguishes a programme's contribution from other influences.

## Components as recorded
Theory-of-change baselining, contribution analysis, cost-benefit analysis, difference-in-differences, sector and workforce mapping, and network mapping using public professional-network data. Source: the register's method row and claim, which record the components and cap our contribution.

## What we can claim
"Contributed sector and workforce mapping and methodology design to the UKRI/AHRC evaluation approach for the Creative Industries Clusters Programme, as subcontractor to BOP Consulting (with Frontier Economics)." The register caps the claim at "contributed to".

## What we cannot claim
- That we designed, owned or implemented the evaluation.
- Any outcome of the evaluation.
- Which component was ours beyond what the register records.

## Limits found in practice
The method row (M-G2-010) is marked PROPOSED, and its fields are shifted by one column (see the repair item in the method package). No application by Fifth Sector exists in the register; any stronger statement needs a project where we ran the approach.

## Fit and gaps for T22-VAIMP
Relevant to "how you will measure impact beyond traditional metrics" and to the IIPP-style ecosystem framing, as design experience only. Not suitable as a lead case study or as evidence of delivery.
