# Blindspot

Apply an adversarial expert perspective to any finding, recommendation or argument.

## Required review

1. Identify the intended audience, decision, incentive and likely hostile reading.
2. Read as a sceptical funder, procurement evaluator, peer reviewer, domain specialist, user, competitor and post-award maintainer where relevant.
3. Surface what the argument assumes, hides, omits, conflates or makes difficult to verify.
4. Challenge evidence quality, provenance, representativeness, rights, cost, delivery, accessibility, operational burden and incentives.
5. Identify alternative interpretations of the evidence and the strongest counterargument.
6. Predict the questions or objections most likely to affect approval, scoring, trust or later delivery.
7. Rank findings by severity and distinguish fatal, material, competitive and presentational risks.
8. Provide specific recovery actions and defensible wording. Do not soften challenges for reassurance.

Use this proactively whenever a recommendation, conclusion or causal argument has significant stakes, especially before external release or funding approval. If multiple expert perspectives are relevant, apply all of them. Do not edit the source unless explicitly authorised.

## Required receipt

Return:

- Frozen argument or recommendation
- Expert perspectives applied
- What a hostile reader notices first
- Hidden assumptions and missing evidence
- Alternative interpretations and counterarguments
- Ranked findings by severity
- Concrete recovery options and trade-offs
- Recommended action
- Re-review gate and residual risk
