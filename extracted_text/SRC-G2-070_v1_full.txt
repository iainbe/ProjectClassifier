Wakefield Creative Skills Needs Assessment Framework and Timetable
Framework Components
1. Sector Analysis
Current state of creative industries in Wakefield
Key subsectors (e.g., film/TV, gaming, music)
Economic contribution and growth trends
2. Skills Supply
Existing education and training provision
Qualification trends
Provider capacity and specialisations
3. Skills Demand
Current skills gaps reported by employers
Projected future skills needs
Impact of technological changes on skills requirements
Impact of changes to regulation and funding of skills
4. Skills Ecosystem
Mapping key stakeholders and their roles
Collaboration mechanisms between industry and education
Policy landscape affecting skills development
5. Skills Development Initiatives
Evaluation of current programmes (e.g., WYCA, accelerator)
Identification of best practices
Gaps in provision
6. Future Skills Scenarios
Potential changes in the creative industries
Impact of broader economic and technological trends
Scenario planning for skills needs
7. Recommendations
Short-term actions to address immediate skills gaps
Long-term strategies for skills development
Policy recommendations for Wakefield Council
 Timetable for Delivery
Phase 1: Inception and Planning (September - October 2024)
September 2024
Meeting with Monika to agree framework elements
Share plan with key stakeholders
Set up data collection systems for ongoing programmes
Finalise assessment framework and methodology
October 2023
Begin stakeholder mapping and engagement 
Initiate interviews with key industry players
Conduct focus groups with education providers and industry representatives
Phase 2: Data Collection and Initial Analysis (November - December 2024)
November 2024
Update analysis of existing data (e.g., FE college courses, numbers and qualification data)
Launch survey of local creative employers
Summarise job postings data for Sep/Oct 24
Conduct in-depth analysis of specific subsectors (e.g., film/TV, gaming)
December 2024
Review outcomes of FE sector review
Analyse summer s’cool and Xccelerator programme evidence
Conduct initial skills gap analysis
Adjust assessment plan if necessary based on policy changes
Prepare interim report on findings to date
Phase 3: Deep Dive and Scenario Planning (January - April 2025)
January - February 2025
Analyse initial WYCA programme data 
Incorporate WYCA programme evaluation results 
Begin future skills scenario development
March - April 2025
Host workshop on future skills scenarios with key stakeholders
Develop draft recommendations
Conduct validation exercises with industry and education stakeholders
Prepare second progress report for Wakefield Council
Phase 4: Synthesis and Reporting (May - June 2025)
May 2025
Synthesise all data and analyses
Develop action plan for implementing recommendations 
Draft full Skills Needs Assessment report
Circulate draft report to key stakeholders for feedback
June 2025
Refine recommendations and action plan based on feedback
Finalise Skills Needs Assessment report
Present findings and recommendations to Wakefield Council
Ongoing Activities (throughout the assessment period)
Monthly calls to review progress, update key findings and provide guidance
Continuous monitoring of policy changes and industry developments that may impact the assessment
Periodic review and adjustment of the assessment framework and timetable as needed
Optional extras
Develop policy recommendations for Wakefield Council/WYCA
Develop comprehensive skills ecosystem map (Wakefield/West Yorkshire)
