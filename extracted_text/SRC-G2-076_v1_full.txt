Proposal Synthesis: The Virtual Agora
Project Title: The Virtual Agora: A Framework for Pluralism and Independent Creation in Europe's Digital Public Sphere
Executive Summary:
The European Union's ambition to foster a sovereign digital public sphere, as articulated in the European Media Freedom Act (EMFA) and the proposed AGORA.EU initiative, represents a pivotal moment for European culture and citizenship. However, this ambition carries a critical risk, as highlighted by key stakeholders such as the European Audiovisual Production association (CEPI), who warn that such initiatives could inadvertently centralise power with established public service media and crowd out the independent creative sector—the very lifeblood of Europe's cultural diversity.
This project, The Virtual Agora, directly confronts this challenge. We will provide the essential research and frameworks to ensure that as Europe's public square extends into immersive virtual worlds, it does so on a foundation of true pluralism and decentralised opportunity.
Our project implements a Tripartite Model to harmonise the interests of Citizens (as Rightsholders), Creators (as Enablers of Rights), and Governance (as the Guardian of the Contract). We will translate the principles of the GDPR and the EU AI Act into the architectural DNA of virtual environments, delivering three core outcomes:
A Predictive Model of Virtual Cultural Evolution: To understand how culture forms and spreads in these new spaces.
The European Framework for Digital Sovereignty: A policy toolkit including a "Charter for Independent Creation" with concrete proposals on algorithmic fairness, discoverability, and economic models that prevent winner-take-all dynamics.
The "Virtual-Ready" Playbook: A practical guide co-developed in Living Labs, with at least one lab established in direct partnership with independent producers to address their specific barriers to entry.
By proactively designing for pluralism, The Virtual Agora will provide the EU with the tools to build a virtual public sphere that is not only innovative and trustworthy but also a vibrant, diverse, and sustainable ecosystem for all European creators.
Agenda: Project Kick-Off & Strategic Alignment (30 Minutes)
Objective: To align all partners on the project's core mission—solving the pluralism challenge for the future digital public sphere—and to define the immediate, high-impact action items.
