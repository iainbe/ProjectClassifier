1. Strategic Objective
Support Greater Manchester’s creative-digital sectors—specifically animation, audio, and studio production—not as isolated content generators, but as vital connective infrastructure. These capabilities underpin immersive delivery, ecosystem resilience, and innovation across sectors including health, education, and manufacturing.

3. Policy Shifts Required (see Section 4.6)
	•	From Output to Infrastructure: Fund the roles and rhythms that make innovation executable, not just the shiny artefacts.
	•	From Gatekeeping to Enablement: Shift universities from central gatekeepers to mesh-aware delivery partners.
	•	From Growth Rhetoric to Resilience Metrics: Track workforce mobility, project interoperability, and network density.
	•	From Digital Hype to Human-Centric Skills: Invest in agile, communicative, and integration-oriented capabilities.
	•	From Legacy Metrics to Ecosystem Intelligence: Use SNA, job data, and live briefs to track real system dynamics.

4. Implementation Priorities (2025–2026)
	•	MITIH to act as an operational testbed for mesh-based innovation funding—microgrants, labs, and placement schemes.
	•	GMCA to pilot mesh enabler residencies and cross-sector crew registries across key federated hubs.
	•	Funders (UKRI, Innovate UK, AHRC) to ringfence funding for audio, studio, and animation as connective infrastructure.
	•	Universities to adopt mesh fluency frameworks in curriculum reform—using job market and SNA evidence to redesign participation pathways.
