2 Our Approach

1. Inception Meeting
At the outset, we will meet with the Project Board to discuss and agree this workplan. We need to be briefed on the different
perspectives on the place narrative held by stakeholders across the park; to identify the shortlist of key stakeholders to be
engaged in one-to-one interviews; and to confirm the list of key audiences and markets, e.g. cultural production, cultural
consumption, sport, leisure, employment, heritage, education, retail, hotels..
2. Expert review of existing branding and messaging
This desk research will rapidly review the existing place narrative and any evidence of previous consultation that the project board
is able to provide us with. We will also review the strategic and planning context for QEOP to establish the scope of proposed
development. We will synthesise existing research on key markets and use available evidence (MOSAIC would be ideal) to
identify and profile target audiences. This may include demographic info on local residents; business travellers (via City Airport,
Stratford Station and visitors to ExCel), and the competition for those audiences.
3. Workshop for core group
This is a critical stage in the study: an opportunity to present evidence from our review, test and confirm shared objectives and key
messages with the core group; and confirm the questions that we want to address through consultation with key stakeholders. At
the workshop, we will:
− Present findings of evidence review.
− Present draft visions and stories for the place narrative, supported by benchmarking against case studies of other similar
  attraction, and suggest scenarios in which these might be used..
− Facilitate discussion to refine the vision and ‘story’.
− Confirm key questions to be addressed through consultation.
4. Stakeholder consultation
We will conduct up to 10 semi-structured interviews (face-to-face or telephone) with key strategic stakeholders who will be
responsible, accountable for or influence development. Interviewees will be agreed at the Inception workshop. We seek a clear
understanding of the key issues and opportunities for the future of QEO Park from strategic stakeholders’ perspectives. BOP will
help to define what aspects of the work should be the focus of the participatory consultation exercise and provide review input as
required. We will use a standard template to capture key data and in-depth accounts of the narrative process.
