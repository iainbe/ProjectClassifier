Title of Requirement: 
ENQ1304 - Derby Culture Strategic Review
Description of Goods / Services / Works Required: 

1. Purpose of the Commission
Derby City Council seeks to appoint a suitably experienced consultant or consortium to undertake a strategic review of Derby’s cultural infrastructure, governance, organisational arrangements and investment model.

The commission will provide evidence-based recommendations to inform future cultural policy, organisational resourcing and investment priorities in the context of local government reorganisation, devolution and wider economic growth ambitions. The review must consider the future role, sustainability and configuration of Derby’s major publicly funded cultural institutions and the contribution of culture to civic, economic and social outcomes.

2. Strategic Context
The commission should be framed in the context of:
Derby City Council Plan 2025 – 28 with particular focus on Vibrant City priorities 
East Midlands Combined County Authority (EMCCA) Inclusive Growth Plan
Arts Council England’s Let’s Create Strategy
Invest 2035: The UK’s Modern Industrial Strategy and Creative Industries Sector Plan
Local Government Reorganisation (LGR)
Derby’s designation as a UNESCO Learning City

3. Objectives of the Consultancy
The appointed consultant will:
Define the civic, economic and social ambitions for culture within Vibrant City priorities
Review Derby’s cultural infrastructure and major institutions 
Recommend future operating and investment models
Identify and propose mechanisms for growing public, private and philanthropic investment
Develop an evidence-based strategic narrative for cultural investment

4. Cultural Infrastructure Review
The consultant must undertake a high-level review of the larger cultural organisations in receipt of public funding. This should provide cost effective options and efficiencies that are practical and deliverable, to ensure a high-quality offer. Focus of the review will be Derby’s building-based organisations – to include but not be limited to the following:
Artcore
Derby Museums
Derby Theatre
Derby LIVE
QUAD

This should include:
Assessment of financial sustainability and strategic alignment
Identify cultural infrastructure gaps and opportunities
Explore options for partnership, collaboration and shared services
Consider governance and operating arrangements and, where appropriate, structural integration

5. Evidence Base and Benchmarking
A comparative benchmarking exercise should be completed using appropriate data sets (to be proposed by the consultant). 

The appointed consultant must:
Benchmark Derby against comparable unitary and metropolitan authorities of similar scale and context (to be proposed by the consultant)
Analyse relative public investment in culture
Assess organisational and funding mix comparators
Identify implications for Derby’s future funding model

6. Cultural Investment Strategy 
A requirement of this commission is the development of an evidence-based cultural investment strategy for Derby. This should establish realistic and appropriate levels of public, private and contributed income required to sustain cultural infrastructure and deliver the defined civic, economic and social outcomes. This should propose practical mechanisms to improve long-term sustainability and reduce overreliance on short-term project funding.

This should be developed with an expectation that future investment models will operate within the context of Local Government Reorganisation and the East Midlands Combined Council Authority.

7. Deliverables
The consultant will be expected to provide:
Defined ambition for culture within Vibrant City priorities
Cultural infrastructure review with options appraisal for governance and operating arrangements
Comparative benchmarking and assessment of implications for Derby
Cultural Investment Strategy 
Policy alignment and implementation roadmap
Strategic narrative that makes the case for cultural investment
Executive summary suitable for members and trustees
Final report suitable for publication and implementation planning

8. Budget
The value of the quotation should not exceed £50,000 excluding VAT.
Review Meetings and Reports:

16 July – commencement of commission with face-to-face inception meeting
Monthly check-in meetings to track progress on the commission.
Late September 2026 – Interim report setting out high level investment proposals, this to inform Council budget setting. 
January 2027 – draft report delivered
February 2027 – final report delivered with presentation to stakeholders
Delivery / Performance Location:

The focus of the commission will be Derby City Centre. Delivery will include meetings with stakeholders and cultural organisations at their premises.
Required Delivery or Completion Date:

It is expected that the commission will be completed by 26 February 2027.
Payment Frequency:

Invoices may be submitted by the Supplier as follows
33% following inception meeting and approval of delivery plan
33% on delivery of interim report
33% on submission of final report.
