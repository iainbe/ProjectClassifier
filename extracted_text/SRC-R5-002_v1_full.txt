Amgen CICP2 Microcluster Evidence Pack
Evidence views for the Wales live-performance, Welsh-language and creative-technology bid
Draft date: 2026-06-20 | Source: Wales evidence universe and Amgen client setup
1. What This Pack Is For
This pack turns the Amgen client setup into reviewable evidence views. It starts from the named live-performance, Welsh-language and creative-technology microclusters in the bid rather than using a national or all-Wales creative-industries baseline as the proof.
The pack is deliberately strict about claim use: core microclusters can carry the delivery story; supporting clusters can only support scale, commercial or language claims where the bid makes that link; review/drop clusters should not be used as bid evidence until pruned.
Evidence Limits
Companies House and company-record evidence do not represent freelancers or informal practitioners.
Nominated seed clusters need partner review before final publication.
Co-investment, letters of support, exact delivery commitments and buyer/adopter commitments remain partner-provided evidence.
Eisteddfod should be represented as itinerant dissemination infrastructure, not a single-location cluster.
Screen and virtual-production records should support commercial spillover and scale only where tied back to live performance and creative technology.
