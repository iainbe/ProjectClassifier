Overview  
Liverpool City Council (the Authority) is seeking quotations from suitably qualified consultants 
to deliver a phased programme of analytical and strategic work  relating to the Liverpool City 
Region Screen Industries . 
This work is being commissioned by Liverpool City Council, with funding contributions from 
the Liverpool City Region Combined Authority . 
The first phase of this commission will deliver a robust Economic Impact Assessment , 
providing a clear, transparent and replicable evidence base to inform policy, investment and 
strategic decision -making.  
The purpose of Phase 1 is to establish this evidence base to inform future decisions. Subject to 
review of Phase 1 outputs, internal approvals and availability of funding, the Authority may 
consider commissioning further phases of work. These may include s trategic analysis, 
development of a Strategic Plan, stakeholder engagement to test priorities, and support for 
Authority -led dissemination.  
The Authority is using this Invitation to Quote to appoint a supplier for Phase 1 only . 
Requirement  
Phase 1 – Economic Impact Assessment (to be commissioned on award)  
The successful supplier will deliver an Economic Impact Assessment of the Liverpool City 
Region Screen Industries  in line with the attached technical brief.  
As a minimum, Phase 1 will be required to:  
• Propose and agree sector definitions, geographies and analytical assumptions with the 
Authority;  
• Be grounded primarily in official, transparent and replicable datasets (e.g. ONS), with 
any supplementary analysis clearly identified, structured and caveated;  
• Estimate the size and structure of the Liverpool City Region Screen Industries in 
absolute and comparative terms;  
• Produce a written report which is capable of standing alone as a complete and robust 
output.  
Completion of Phase 1 will act as a formal review point  for the Authority.

Potential Subsequent Phases (subject to review and approval)  
Following completion and review of Phase 1, the Authority may choose to commission further 
phases of work , which could include, but are not limited to:  
• Development of a Strategic Plan for the Screen Industries;  
• Stakeholder engagement to inform and test strategic priorities;  
• Iterative development and refinement of strategic options and interventions;  
• Support to Authority -led dissemination or workshops.  
The scope, sequencing, scale and procurement approach for any subsequent phases will be 
determined by the Authority following Phase 1 review . The Authority is not committing, 
through this ITQ, to commission any subsequent phases of work.

Monitoring and Review  
Delivery of Phase 1 will be managed through proportionate contract management 
arrangements. Completion of Phase 1 will act as a formal review point, informing decisions on 
whether to proceed with further phases, their scope, budget availability and the app ropriate 
procurement route.  
Budget  
The Authority currently anticipates that up to £38,000 (exclusive of VAT)  may be available to 
support this work across all phases , subject to internal approvals and availability of funding. 
This budget reflects contributions from Liverpool City Council and the Liverpool City Region 
Combined Authority.  
This Invitation to Quote relates only to Phase 1 , for which tenderers are required to submit a 
fixed price quotation . 
Appointment of a supplier for Phase 1 does not guarantee  that the same supplier will be 
engaged to deliver any subsequent phases. The Authority reserves the right to determine the 
appropriate procurement route for any future phases, including re -tendering, depending on the 
scope, value and applicable procuremen t regulations.  
Tenderers should indicate whether they are VAT registered.
