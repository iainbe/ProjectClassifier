Debrief information
Please find below debrief information in relation to your response and the reasons for the decision.
Quality
Strengths: relevant international experience across creative economy strategy, cultural valuation, creative industries mapping, British Council commissions, economic impact assessment and innovation ecosystems. 

Weaknesses: Limited evidence of large-scale national evaluation programmes. Limited demonstrated experience in formal econometric impact evaluation.
Methodology and Approach
Strengths: The methodology is built around the British Council’s own framework from Why Technology Needs Artists, and it shows understanding of causal evaluation. The panel scored positively the fact that it examines: knowledge diffusion, network effects, collaboration formation, product spillovers and convergence-economy effects. 

Weaknesses: The proposal lacks clarity: it mentions a desire to understand economic impact but doesn't clearly state how it will undertake this work.
Commercial
