13 ANNUAL MODEL JUDGEMENT

# The largest uncertainties are not hidden in the multiplier

The source base supports an estimate but cannot directly observe every counterfactual parameter.

|  |  |
| :-: | :-: |
| What the evidence showsThe source-informed 20% allocation reserve gives £66.9m. Restoring the saved 30% reserve gives £58.5m; removing it gives £83.6m. These are conditional geographic cases, not a confidence interval. Inbound engagement and the expenditure-to-GVA coefficient also matter: the illustrated 65% inbound-engagement case gives £86.6m, holding other inputs fixed. | How to read itTown expenditure is destination-attributed, but GBTS includes travel, advance purchases and packages; the inbound basket also uses a regional proxy. A zero reserve therefore overstates precision. Conversely, importing the Eurovision survey’s roughly 28% outside-area adjustment unchanged would ignore differences in what GBTS/IPS already exclude. The revised 20% is a cautious judgement, with 0% and 30% retained as tests—not a measured correction. |
