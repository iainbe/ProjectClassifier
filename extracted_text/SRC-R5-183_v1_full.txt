Introduction
BOP is working with LLDC and a working group of stakeholders in and around Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park to produce a new place narrative for the Park - one which reflects the views, needs and aspirations of its users and stakeholders. We would greatly value your input to this work.
This is a critical time in the development of the Park, and in the realisation of the Olympic legacy for communities and businesses across East London. 
The Park, its use and the way in which it is perceived are being reshaped by existing communities, businesses, visitors, new housing development and the growth of employment, education and cultural provision. These factors combine to present the need to update existing messages and join them together through a single place narrative.
The place narrative will be one element of a brand strategy for The Park that provides a framework for its use in a range of different contexts, in partnership with its diverse stakeholders and investors.
