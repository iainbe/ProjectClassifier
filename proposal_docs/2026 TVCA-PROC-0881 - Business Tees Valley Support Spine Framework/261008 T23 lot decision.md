## The tender

TVCA is procuring a framework of seven **single-supplier lots**, £10m maximum total, contract 2 years + 2-year extension. All lots are rate-card service delivery: workshops, coaching hours, sprints, incubator/accelerator cohorts, market development interventions. No guaranteed volume — activity is called off.

Written tender scores (Quality 60 / Price 20 / Social Value 20, minimum score 3 per question) **only shortlist the top three per lot**. The presentation stage is scored fresh and alone decides the award. The written bid is a ticket to the room.

## The two survivors

### Lot 4 — Creative and Cultural Industries

- Buys a sole supplier running creative-sector business support across all pathways and tiers; mandated ongoing dialogue with Teesside University (Investment Zone research partner); must complement NE Screen Industries Development Programme, Sonic Futures and a forthcoming IP Fund
- Our strength: P46 prior TVCA creative economy work, P68 WY Create live combined-authority business support programme, the wider creative economy corpus
- Our gap: venues, caseload management, Tees Valley presence, delivery bench at scale — exposed at the presentation stage, which decides the award
- **Verdict: bid only inside a consortium.** The ITT expressly allows collaborative submissions. Shape: delivery partner runs operations, we bring sector evidence, impact measurement and the "connected system" answer to Q1. No named co-bidder by ~16/10 means drop it

### Lot 7 — Specialist Expertise Network

- Buys a coordinated network of specialist mentors, coaches, consultants and technical experts, deployed hourly across all lots wherever Account Managers identify a need — covering every sector, not just creative
- Our strength: the associate-bench model is what we already run; the two bespoke quality questions (establishing and assuring the network; matching business need to expertise responsively) suit a structured matching-method answer competitors can't easily tell
- Our gap: the roster must cover general business expertise, not just creative — the bench needs assembling and naming now; PI £2m required (held: £5m, covered)
- **Verdict: prime-worthy.** The only lot where smallness helps — the buyer wants a coordinator of expertise. FVRA turnover ratio also stays safer at Lot 7's hourly-rate scale
