## 3. Questions to expect from Pete, with candid replies

### "Why can't Places do this for the original amount?"

Places can reduce repeat assembly, classification, exploration and reporting effort where those capabilities and sources are available. The fee still has to cover the commissioned work: definitions, data access, validation, interviews, economic measurement, benchmarking, recommendations and quality assurance.

Bring a task-and-output cost account. Do not substitute a universal capability claim or a vague assertion that it is a large job.

### "I need a strategy, not another scoping report."

Agree the destination. Offer a first result that already informs a decision and leaves usable data, rather than a generic methodology document. If the full strengthening framework is the mandatory immediate output, quote a proportionate full approach through the procurement's permitted route.

Do not call Stage A a complete strategy. A subsequent decision gate is a genuine choice, not a promise of later work.

### "What exactly would I get for roughly GBP 20,000?"

Propose the Stage A product in section 4, then cost it against actual capacity, data and engagement. The current evidence supports an outline, rather than a binding quotation. Confirm VAT, scope, source charges, timetable, sign-off and editable outputs.

Avoid saying "all of the mapping" when some required measures, interviews or records remain outside the priced deliverable.

### "Can AI do the heavy lifting?"

It can assist extraction, classification, synthesis and repeatable tasks where the workflow is verified. Accuracy, source rights, hidden gaps, human validation and defensible decisions remain delivery obligations. Specify which tasks are assisted, what is reviewed and how failures are handled.

No productivity saving, full automation or delivered Liverpool dataset has been demonstrated in this review.

### "Can you give me the GVA, exports, employment and productivity numbers?"

For each measure, identify the population, location basis, year, source and claim state. Where a compatible measure exists, use it. Where it is modelled, show the basis and uncertainty. Where it cannot be defended, provide a clear coverage gap and recovery option.

Regional economic measures can exist while remaining unsuitable for a particular digital subset. Company turnover, registered-office employment, advertised vacancies and grant awards cannot simply be substituted for GVA, workplace jobs, workforce demand or investment.

### "Who is in the digital cluster?"

Agree a scope that separates firms building relevant technology, domain-specific technology products, enabling/supplier capabilities and adopters. Keep a shared organisation identity across overlapping labels; show counts without double counting.

The ITT's themes are the field to investigate, rather than proof of locally established clusters. Give reasons for inclusion and uncertainty; classification beyond SIC needs evidence and a review route.

### "Can you assess the assets and say what interventions will work?"

Assess documented services, users, partnerships and outcomes, supported by targeted engagement. Distinguish provision from use, use from benefit, and reported benefit from causal impact. Prioritise recommendations with mechanism, evidence, owner, cost/funding route and risks.

A map or inferred edge does not establish an asset's additional contribution or demonstrate that a proposed intervention causes growth.

### "Why not use the research we already have?"

We should. The ITT promises initial desk research and data. Recover and assess them first, with rights, dates, definitions and gaps recorded. Price the new work rather than charging to rebuild an existing base.

Where previous studies disagree, explain the population or method difference before asserting one is wrong. Previous creative, fintech and life-science work is context rather than automatic coverage of the expanded technology remit.

### "How does this become a ten-year framework?"

Through validated priorities, stakeholder input and costed, owned actions. The first phase clarifies the evidence and decisions needed; later work supplies the deeper assessment and intervention case where required.

The framework should distinguish near-term action from hypotheses needing research, with review points rather than unsupported ten-year forecasts. If the contract requires the whole framework now, reflect that work in the cost and delivery plan.

### "Will I have to keep paying you to use the results?"

The contract should specify editable deliverables and appropriate reuse rights for LCRCA. Separate The Fifth Sector background platform IP from commissioned outputs and provider restrictions. Any continued hosting, subscriptions, new data or refresh service should be transparent, optional where the contract permits, and separately priced.

No platform access licence, continuing fee or indefinite free support is agreed in the reviewed material.

### "Can a partner fill the specialist gaps?"

Potentially, with a defined role, price, capacity, licence and acceptance condition. The Oxford Economics conversation is a proposed study, not an agreed subcontract. University or data-provider involvement also needs actual approval.

Bring confirmed capability and partner commitments. Disclose any material connection when evaluating strategic assets; a relationship can provide access without substituting for balanced assessment.

### "Other bidders will offer the full job at that price."

Evaluate our own defensible scope and economics. Existing data, different engagement depth or efficiencies can change costs. Do not speculate about rivals' honesty. Ask for the common scope and acceptance rules, then decide whether to bid.

If the full requirements are fixed and the fee cannot support responsible delivery, decline or seek a permitted clarification. Avoid subsidising an unbounded commission for a hoped-for future partnership.

### The broader sector scope changes the method, not just configuration

### Four decision gates, with a real choice at each

Gate 1: define and establish the baseline. Decide whether the scope, available evidence, capabilities and buyer decision justify a deeper study. Stage A supports this gate.

Gate 2: validate the ecosystem and priority opportunities. Undertake the justified economic, skills, connectivity, asset, finance and comparator work. Decide which opportunities have sufficiently credible evidence to develop.

Gate 3: develop intervention and investment cases. Establish mechanisms, demand, delivery partners, costs, funding dependencies and risks. Decide which actions merit commitment and what remains a research hypothesis.

Gate 4: agree the strengthening framework and operating arrangements. Set priorities, owners, sequencing, governance, success measures and review points, with a separate decision on evidence maintenance.

These are management decision points, not four guaranteed contracts or a mandatory new programme. If LCRCA commissions the full work once, they can sit inside one contract with appropriately scoped milestone payments and acceptance.

### Cost the work before setting a fixed fee

The 2 October transcript's calculation at 31:48 needs correcting: GBP 20,000 divided by GBP 700 per person-day is approximately 28.6 person-days, rather than 20. These are person-days across the whole delivery team, not elapsed working days for each member.

These are sensitivity illustrations, not our agreed rate, a quote, a VAT determination or evidence of actual project cost. A supplier rate must fund delivery, management, review and business overhead. Data, travel, engagement and support need explicit allocation.

To price the first offer, cost definition/source review; data preparation and classifications; mapping/registers; validation; synthesis/recommendations; management/QA; licences and other expenses; and risk allowance. Credit usable existing work instead of assuming a ground-up rebuild.

Then check that the actual contracted outputs fit the available capacity and margin. The initial budget should not rely on unlimited founder time, an unpriced partner or later contracts recovering a loss.
