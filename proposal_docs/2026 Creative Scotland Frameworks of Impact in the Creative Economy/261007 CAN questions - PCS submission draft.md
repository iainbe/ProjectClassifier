Should the framework prioritise impacts measurable within Creative Scotland's funding footprint, or cover the wider creative economy including embedded creative work?

2. The framework is to align with Creative Scotland's refreshed strategy, which is in development, and with the draft National Performance Framework outcomes. Will draft strategy directions or NPF proposals be shared with the appointed supplier, or under embargo with bidders?

4. What route will be used to share funding award data and Multi Year Funded Organisation annual data (for example extract, secure environment or direct system access), when would access begin after contract start, and what data protection requirements should bidders plan and price for?

5. Does "aggregating qualitative information" mean synthesising qualitative material that already exists (for example evaluations, consultation responses and Our Creative Voice material) within this commission, or designing methods for future primary collection?

6. Is this work expected to feed directly into the new Performance Management Framework and KPI set, and if so which team within Creative Scotland owns that workstream?

7. What form does Creative Scotland expect the framework to take (for example an indicator dictionary and technical specification for internal use, or a published framework for sector use), and who is the primary intended user?

8. Is implementation or a further phase anticipated beyond the contract end date of 30 April 2027, and should the framework be designed for handover to Creative Scotland staff or for a further commissioned build?

9. Where a tender is submitted as a consortium, do the insurance requirements in the Instructions to Tenderers apply to each member of the consortium, or is cover held by the consortium lead sufficient?
