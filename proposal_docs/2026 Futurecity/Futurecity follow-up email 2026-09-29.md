You asked what we could do if a client approached you tomorrow with a scheme on a blank page. As we discussed, the first step to establishing that could be a back-test using Wembley as the test case. Testing Places on the Wembley scheme would show what our data finds about the changes to the place and to the creative economy around it, and give you a method and evidence to show prospective clients what value the measurement of impact could add before anyone commits to the full work. The attached note sets out a proposed outline of the test, with an appendix setting out benefits to potential clients.

The test:

- Draws on our data on Wembley's creative businesses since 2016, official business and employment figures for Brent and the local context Places adds, alongside the material you already hold.
- Delivers, within two weeks of commission, an evidence note with one visual, a sketch of the interventions, up to five metrics worth measuring, a partner data request, a baseline checklist for your next scheme and an approach and method statement that you can use in pitches and discussions of new projects.
- Suggested fee: £5k. Illustrative costs for other types of project are included in a second appendix.
- Excludes buying data, interviews and any claim about cause. Where your records have gaps, we will provide a list of what data to ask partners for.
