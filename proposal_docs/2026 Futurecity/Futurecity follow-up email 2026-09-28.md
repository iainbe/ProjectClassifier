You asked what you would do first if a client started a scheme tomorrow on a blank page. We've had a go at answering that from our side. The attached note sets out what we would build for you, and the ten questions a client is most likely to ask. For each one it shows what our Places tool already holds, what else we would need, and what the client would get: jobs, GVA, number of businesses, change in footfall and so on. I also attach a shorter version without the Wembley notes, which you can share with clients.

We suggest starting with a test at Wembley for £5k. We would put our own data on Wembley's creative businesses alongside the evidence, deck and figures you already hold. You would come away with two things: a short note of what our data finds at Wembley, and a description of the approach and method to use in pitches. That lets you show prospective clients what the measurement adds before anyone commits to the full work.

The test works from the material you already hold and our own data. Where your records have gaps, we turn them into a short list of what to ask partners for, and the fee stays at £5k. Buying data, interviews and any claim about cause belong to the scheme work.

When a client appoints you on a scheme, the measurement would start from £20k in the UK, where the baseline statistics are readily available. That covers a map of the local creative economy and its trend, comparable cases and counterfactuals tailored to the scheme, a forecast with a confidence level for each part of the proposition, and the baseline itself.

Footfall, rents and visitor surveys would be priced with the client where the data does not already exist. Overseas we would expect the fee to be 1.5 to 2.5 times the UK figure, because more of the source data needs collecting, and we would set it in proportion to the total value of the project.

Many clients will already have projections of rents, retail sales or other turnover. We can translate those into GVA, which public sector investors need for a business case. A social return on investment study is available as an option.

Once a scheme is under way, we would work alongside you and the client through delivery and after opening, with reviews at opening, one year and two years. We would agree that fee case by case.

All fees exclude VAT, travel, data licences, incentives for survey respondents and legal advice. Before we rely on any data a partner shares, we check its source, dates, area and permitted use.

For the Wembley test, it would help to have the deck and the narrative for each slide, your shortlist of what made the difference, and any figures you already hold on footfall, rents, tenancy or hire income. It would also help to know who the note is for, and which partners you're happy for us to name.

It would also be good to find an hour in the next few weeks to talk through how the associate arrangement might work.

Let me know what you think. If you're happy to go ahead, send the deck over when it's ready and we'll get started.
