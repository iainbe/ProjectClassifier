# Iain Bennett FRSA: Brief Biography

**Role on this commission:** Valuation and appraisal lead. Costing design, KPI and evaluation architecture, delivery and governance options, prioritisation, technical inputs.

---

Iain Bennett is the founder of The Fifth Sector, with more than 30 years' experience in creative sector strategy, economic development and applied research. His work emphasises the leading role of culture and the creative industries in the renewal and growth of places, spaces and economies.

## Relevant delivered work

**Wakefield Cultural Development Fund evaluation (2022 to 2025).** Led the evaluation of Wakefield Council's CDF programme (2020 to 2024), including Phase 2. Built the Theory of Change (adapting the DCMS national CDF ToC), the evaluation framework (v3.0), KPIs across economic, place, leadership and skills outcomes, and the impact framework. This is a place based cultural programme evaluation directly comparable to Belfast's 6.6 requirement.

**Wakefield Our Year 24 evaluation (2024).** Led the evaluation of Wakefield's year long cultural programme, with its own Theory of Change and Connected Strategic Aims. Interim and final evaluation reports delivered.

**Liverpool City Region Music Economy Report.** Designed and implemented the music economy evaluation with the University of Liverpool's Institute of Popular Music, converting the music economy's £406m GVA into a £780m total economic impact figure. A published, worked multiplier calculation in a creative economy context.

**Liverpool Film Fund evaluation.** Led the evaluation of the Liverpool Film Fund.

**True North CICP (in principle, if bid funded).** Named as evaluation lead in WP6 (Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning) on Lancaster University's AHRC Creative Industries Cluster Programme Round 2 bid. If funded, the 60 month programme starts January 2027.

**Creative sector mapping and cluster strategies.** In the last five years has delivered creative sector mapping in Liverpool, Manchester, West Yorkshire, South Yorkshire, Tees Valley, Derby, Leicester, West Sussex, Greater Brighton, Surrey, Hampshire, Somerset, Herefordshire and North East Scotland (for Aberdeen City Council).

## Earlier career

- **Sector Leader for Digital & Creative Industries, Northwest Regional Development Agency (2006 to 2011).** Wrote the first regional strategy for digital and creative industries; managed skills and infrastructure programmes supporting the £1 billion development of MediaCityUK in Salford.
- **Associate Director, BOP Consulting (2014 to 2019).** Wrote an international Creative Industries strategy for UKTI (now DBT); advised Festivals Edinburgh on international best practice in digital festivals technology; devised strategies for the computer games cluster in Leamington Spa and the fashion cluster in London's Olympic Park.
- **Agent for actors and directors** at international talent agencies including William Morris and ICM.
