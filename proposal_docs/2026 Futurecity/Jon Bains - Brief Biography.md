# Jon Bains: Brief Biography

**Role on this commission:** Places data architecture. Places deployment, comparator construction, measurement architecture, monitoring tools, option register build.

---

Jon Bains is the founder and lead of Places, The Fifth Sector's evidence platform for creative economy and place decisions, built on source tagged, labelled evidence architecture.

## Relevant delivered work

**Places evidence platform.** Built and leads Places, which applies source tagging, denominators and explicit evidence labelling to every dataset it holds. Every figure is traceable to its origin; every claim is tagged by what it is: observed amount, descriptive estimate, scenario estimate, cost, benefit or economic baseline.

**AHRC Creative Industries Cluster Programme bids.** Delivered Places evidence for two CICP bids:
- **AMGEN**, led by the University of South Wales
- **True North**, led by Lancaster University (submitted 22 July 2026; if funded, Places becomes the foundation of the evaluation model, with every input and intervention tracked at LSOA level)

**Creative industries mapping.** Places underpins current creative industries mapping for Aberdeen City Council (drawing on Skills Development Scotland data) and has delivered mapping across Liverpool and Surrey.

**Places deployments.** Led Places data architecture, comparator evidence, toolkit construction and network mapping across multiple CICP and place mapping deployments. The toolkit makes evidence usable by people who are not economists, a design principle carried into Belfast.

## Earlier career

More than 25 years designing and governing digital products for organisations including BBC Radio 1, Which?, CIPD and Amnesty International. Founder of Obsolete.com, developing accessible approaches to AI adoption.

## What Places brings to Belfast

For Belfast, Places is our internal tool for structuring cost and evaluation data. It provides:

- **Source tagged cost evidence**: every figure traceable to its origin
- **Comparator construction**: benchmark performance from the lead's 6.2 case studies and Places' UK wide place dataset
- **Measurement architecture**: source tagging, denominators and evidence labelling applied to every KPI in 6.6
- **Monitoring dashboard scaffold**: practical tools usable by people who are not economists
- **Governed data structure**: costed pipeline held in a structure that integrates with the investment framework (6.9) and hands off to the lead's GIS (6.10)

We hand outputs to the lead for integration into Maritime Belfast's own GIS.
